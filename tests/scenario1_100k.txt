Run-Length Encoding (RLE) compresses consecutive identical bytes. Huffman coding constructs optimal variable-length prefix codes based on character frequencies. Lempel-Ziv-Welch (LZW) builds a dynamic dictionary of recurring sub-strings during a single pass. Deflate combines LZ77 sliding window dictionary matching with Huffman entropy coding to achieve high compression efficiency.
